# 🔥 ˗ˏˋ EMBÊR ˎˊ˗ 🍽️  #
Welcome to **Embêr**, a fine dining fusion restaurant and sustainability-driven experience. Our mission is to **blend bold flavors** from Indian, Chinese, and Filipino cuisines with **cutting-edge eco-conscious practices** right from farm to fork.
## 🌱 Sustainability  ## 
 ☆ 💧We grow produce in **hydroponic greenhouses**, using 99% less land and 98% less water. <br>
 
 ☆ 🚜 Our upstate NY farm supplies **fresh ingredients year-round** from tropical fruits to dairy and eggs. <br>
 
 ☆ ♻️ Food scraps are **composted and reused**, creating a closed-loop system that supports both plants and animals.
